# The Periodic Table of Robotics

Chemistry has 118 elements. Robotics, it turns out, has **86** — and you're
looking at all of them. Every topic, skill and area of robotics, arranged
the way Mendeleev would have wanted: the fundamentals on the left, the
clever stuff on the right, and a whole row of actual robots along the
bottom (the *Botinoids* — the rare earths of the maker world).

Click any element to learn what it is, why it matters, and where to go
next — each one links to related courses, projects and articles here on
KevsRobots.

{% include periodic_table.html %}

> **How to read the table:** the atomic number is the suggested learning
> order *within a column of the same colour* — start with `Oh` (Ohm's Law,
> element 1) and work your way towards `Ai` (element 2, top right — easy to
> reach, hard to master). Colours group related disciplines, just like the
> real table groups metals and noble gases. And no, we couldn't resist
> making `Pb` the symbol for PCB design.

Found an element we missed? [Let me know](/about/) — the table, like
robotics itself, is still expanding.
